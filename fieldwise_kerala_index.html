About this prototype

FieldWise Kerala is a bilingual learning prototype. It combines NASA weather information with farmer-entered details to show illustrative crop-rotation options.

These are educational examples, not validated agronomic recommendations. Please confirm crop choices with your local Krishi Bhavan or agricultural officer.
